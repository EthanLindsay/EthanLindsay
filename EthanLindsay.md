## About

Final-year **Bachelor of Computing (Software Engineering)** student at Belgium Campus ITversity, based in Cape Town, South Africa.

Alongside my degree I co-founded and run **CTRLWEB**, a web development company delivering client work across South Africa since 2023 — everything from design systems and CMS architecture to hosting and deployment. Running it commercially has taught me as much about scoping, client communication and shipping to a deadline as the degree has about algorithms.

Most of what I build sits somewhere between the backend and the interface. I like problems where the data model and the thing the user actually touches both have to be right.

- 🎓 Graduating **December 2027** — the degree includes a full year of industry placement
- 💼 Available for **workplace placement, January–December 2027**
- 🔨 Currently working with Node.js, C#, and Unreal Engine 5
- 🎨 400+ hours in Blender, which is how the C++ habit started
